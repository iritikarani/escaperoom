Privacy Policy

Escape Room bot (@escapethisroombot) · Last updated: 31 October 2026

What we store

  • Your Telegram user ID, first and last name, and username.
  • The chats where you play: your private chat with the bot, and the group of your team in 2v2 matches.
  • Game data: matches you join, your team, the answers you submit, hints used, results and match times.
  • Which puzzles you have already seen, so future matches can give you new ones.

Why we store it

Only to run the game: matchmaking, keeping score, telling your opponent which room you have reached, and choosing puzzles you haven't played before.

Who can see it

Your data is never sold or shared with anyone. Your opponent sees only your display name, which room you have reached, and the final result. They never see your answers, guesses or hints. In 2v2 matches your teammate sees your guesses, because you play together.

Where it is kept

On the bot's server, hosted by PythonAnywhere. Messages pass through Telegram as with any bot, and Telegram's own privacy policy applies to them.

How long we keep it

Game records are kept so your history and results stay available. Technical records of incoming Telegram updates are deleted after 7 days.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time by blocking it in Telegram. To ask for your data to be deleted, CONTACT_LINE
